Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 Compliant with Applicable Consumer Laws

1. Scope & Consumer Protection Framework

This Refund & Cancellation Policy sets forth the terms and conditions under which refunds and order adjustments are processed for participants on the PlayStation Events platform.

This policy is formulated in compliance with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and relevant electronic commerce rules in India. Nothing contained herein is intended to diminish, restrict, or override any mandatory statutory rights granted to consumers under applicable Indian laws.

2. Event Cancellation by the Organizer

If an event tier is officially cancelled by the organizer prior to winner selection due to technical, regulatory, or administrative circumstances:

  • Full Refund Entitlement: All eligible registered participants who acquired tickets for the cancelled event will receive a 100% full refund of the entry amount paid.
  • Notification Process: The organizer will notify affected participants via the registered mobile number and email address, detailing the cancellation and refund steps.
  • Method of Refund: Refunds are credited directly back to the original payment instrument (bank account, card, or UPI VPA) utilized during ticket purchase.

3. Policy for Completed & Active Events

If an event proceeds as announced and is conducted in accordance with disclosed schedules:

  • General Non-Refundability: Under the organizer's proposed event rules, once a ticket entry has been confirmed and allocated an active serial in the event pool, tickets are generally non-refundable.
  • Allocation Finality: Because capacity slots are limited and immutably assigned, completed entries cannot be retracted after entry allocation, subject to applicable laws and verifiable technical exceptions.

4. Distinction: Cancellation vs. Voluntary Withdrawal

It is important to clearly distinguish between an organizer-initiated event cancellation and a participant's voluntary choice:

  • Organizer Event Cancellation: Qualifies for a full refund as detailed in Section 2 above.
  • Participant Voluntary Withdrawal: A participant's voluntary decision not to participate, change of mind, or post-purchase regret after valid ticket confirmation does not constitute grounds for a refund under standard promotional event guidelines.

5. Duplicate Debits & Failed Order Processing

If a participant experiences technical billing anomalies:

  • Duplicate Deductions: If multiple payments are deducted for a single order submission due to network latency, duplicate charges will be reviewed and refunded upon verification.
  • Payment Debited Without Ticket Allocation: If funds are debited from a user's account but the platform server fails to generate a valid ticket allocation, the transaction will be reviewed against gateway logs and processed for return in accordance with standard payment provider procedures.
Claiming Duplicate Review: Participants should retain their transaction SMS, Bank UTR reference, and order timestamp, and notify support within 7 calendar days of the occurrence.

6. Refund Processing Timeline & Method

To maintain strict transparency, we do not claim unverified or instantaneous refund timelines:

  • Once an authorized refund is approved following review of transaction records, the reversal instruction is initiated directly to the original payment source.
  • The actual crediting of funds into the user's account is subject to interbank clearing switches and payment provider clearing schedules (typically taking between 5 to 7 standard business days).
