Winner Selection & Result Announcement

Effective Date: November 2026 Documented Video Selection Protocol

1. Selection Methodology in Simple Language

We believe that promotional prize events must be simple, open, and understandable for every participant. Our selection process is designed so that every eligible ticket purchased has an equal and fair opportunity of being selected.

Here is how the selection works in 4 simple steps:

  1. Entry Allocation: When you purchase tickets for an event, each ticket is assigned a sequential numerical ticket number (for example, Ticket #1 through Ticket #6,000).
  2. Entry Verification: Before the draw begins, the total list of valid, confirmed ticket numbers for that specific event is verified and locked.
  3. Random Number Generation: A true random number generator selects the winning ticket number from the active pool of numbers.
  4. Winner Matching & Recording: The selected ticket number is matched to the participant who registered it, and the result is officially documented.

2. Proposed Use of Random.org for Fair Selection

To generate unbiased random numbers, the organizer proposes to utilize Random.org, a globally recognized atmospheric noise randomness service, subject to a documented and lawful process.

During the selection, the range of numbers (from 1 to the total number of confirmed entries for that event) is entered into the Random.org service to generate the winning serial number.

Important Transparency Disclosure: Random.org is an independent online computational tool used solely for generating random values. Random.org does not independently audit, verify, endorse, or sponsor this event, the organizer, or the participant entries. We do not claim any third-party audit or certification that does not genuinely exist.

3. Event Timeline & 12:00 AM IST Schedule

Event cycles operate on a structured schedule aligned with India Standard Time (IST, UTC+05:30):

  • Result Scheduled Time: Daily event results are scheduled for 12:00 AM (Midnight IST) according to the active event countdown timer displayed on each card.
  • Pool Closure: Ticket participation closes at the end of the scheduled cycle or immediately upon reaching 100% capacity, whichever occurs first. No new entries are accepted once the selection cycle initiates.

4. Documented Video Publication on Official Instagram

To ensure complete public transparency and verifiable documentation, the organizer intends to record a continuous, unedited screen and audio video capturing the entire selection process.

The video will document:

  • The total count of participating entries in the event pool.
  • The real-time input of entry ranges into the Random.org selection interface.
  • The generation of the winning number.
  • The live matching of the selected number against the participant entry record.

The organizer intends to publish this documentation video on its official Instagram profile:

Official Social Media Profile:
Instagram Channel: [Official Instagram Profile — Link will be updated here prior to event close]
Please note: The account link will be officially published prior to the conclusion of the event cycle. The selection procedure and video publication must correspond strictly to the actual process conducted.

5. Winner Notification & Prize Claim Instructions

Following the result selection, the following verification and delivery protocol is initiated:

  • Direct Communication: The organizer contacts the selected participant within 24 to 48 hours via phone call and SMS to the registered 10-digit mobile number, and via official email.
  • Claim Period: The selected participant has a designated period of 7 calendar days from the initial notice to respond and confirm their claim.
  • Identity Verification: To prevent fraud and ensure lawful distribution, the claimant must provide valid government-issued photo identification (Aadhaar card, PAN card, or Voter ID) matching the legal name registered at the time of entry.
  • Doorstep Delivery Coordination: Once verified, the brand-new PlayStation hardware prize is dispatched via insured courier service with trackable shipping to the winner's residential address in India.

6. Ineligible or Unclaimed Entries Protocol

To ensure event completion in cases where a selected person cannot receive the prize:

  • If a selected participant cannot be reached after multiple documented contact attempts over 7 calendar days; or
  • If the selected participant is found to be legally ineligible under applicable state laws, provided false contact details, or fails to produce matching identity verification:

The entry is recorded as forfeited, and the organizer may conduct a documented secondary random selection following the identical transparent procedure, or reschedule the prize allocation as disclosed in advance.

7. Integrity & Process Transparency

We are committed to operating all prize events with the highest standard of procedural integrity:

  • No employees, immediate family members, or organizers are eligible to participate or win prizes.
  • Selection records, timestamps, and video files are preserved in official archives for regulatory and compliance review.
  • Participants can review their participation details at any time using the "Get Verified Ticket & Prize" feature available in the website header.
