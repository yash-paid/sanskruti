Who We Are

A Digital Library Born from a Passion for Knowledge

Sanskruti eBooks is an Indian digital publishing and e-commerce venture dedicated to creating and distributing high-quality eBooks in the fields of self-improvement, mental health, holistic wellness, Ayurveda, fitness, and personal growth.

We believe that life-transforming knowledge should be affordable, accessible, and instantly available to every Indian — whether you live in a metro city or a smaller town. That is why all our titles are offered as instant digital downloads at prices far below conventional print books, with full support in both Hindi and English.

Our eBooks are carefully crafted to include actionable protocols, real science-backed insights, and practical wisdom drawn from modern neuroscience and traditional Indian systems like Ayurveda and Yoga. We exist to serve the millions of Indians who are hungry for genuine, transformative knowledge at an accessible price.
