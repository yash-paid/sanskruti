Payment Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 Transparent Billing Guidelines

1. Upfront Pricing & Transparent Disclosure

At PlayStation Events, all participation fees are transparent and explicitly declared prior to the commencement of any order. There are zero hidden costs, processing markups, or recurring subscriptions.

Ticket pricing across all 12 events is standardized according to console tier and prize configuration:

  • PlayStation 5 Category: Event 1 (₹10) • Event 2 (₹20) • Event 3 (₹50) • Event 4 (₹100) • Event 5 (₹200) • Event 6 (₹300).
  • PlayStation 5 Pro Category: Event 1 (₹15) • Event 2 (₹25) • Event 3 (₹60) • Event 4 (₹115) • Event 5 (₹225) • Event 6 (₹325).

2. Dynamic Total Amount Calculation

Before confirming participation, users review the complete transaction breakdown inside the participation modal and pre-payment summary:

  • Unit Price Display: The cost per individual ticket is displayed next to the selected event title.
  • Quantity Selection: Users choose their desired quantity within the allowable remaining event capacity.
  • Total Calculation: The payable amount dynamically recalculates in real-time as:
    Total Amount = Unit Ticket Price × Selected Quantity

The participant is shown the exact final total before being asked to confirm their contact information or proceed to payment.

3. Supported Payment Instruments & Operational Status

The platform is engineered to support standard domestic Indian payment instruments:

  • UPI: Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and bank UPI apps.
  • Net Banking: Major scheduled Indian commercial banks.
  • Debit / Credit Cards: Domestic RuPay, Visa, and Mastercard cards issued in India.
Current Operating Disclosure: Live payment collection for chance-based prize events requires specific merchant regulatory underwriting. Live payment gateway processing is currently scheduled for rollout upon completion of final statutory formalities. No unauthorized charges or real bank fund debits occur while the platform is in pre-launch integration status.

4. Payment Confirmation & Order Identifiers

Upon successful completion of a transaction:

  • An official Order Reference Number (format: ORD-PS-XXXXX) is issued to identify the transaction.
  • Each participating ticket purchased is allocated a unique numerical entry serial for inclusion in the daily event pool.
  • An electronic confirmation summary displaying the event name, ticket quantity, amount paid, and timestamp is made available for the participant's records.

5. Handling of Failed, Pending, or Duplicate Transactions

During online transactions, intermittent banking server timeouts or mobile connection drops may occasionally affect transaction status:

  • Failed Transactions: If funds are debited from your bank account but the transaction fails to complete, Indian banking switch standards typically auto-reverse the funds back to your account within 3 to 5 business days.
  • Pending Transactions: If your payment status remains pending with your bank, the system waits for automated webhook confirmation before issuing ticket serials. If confirmation is not received within the settlement window, the transaction is reversed.
  • Duplicate Charges: In the rare instance that a user is charged more than once for a single order submission due to network latency, duplicate debits are verified against payment logs and processed for prompt reversal in accordance with our Refund Policy.
