Plain Language Summary: Sanskruti eBooks collects your WhatsApp number and name to deliver your purchased eBooks. We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not store your payment credentials. You can request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us on WhatsApp.

1 Who We Are

This Privacy Policy applies to Sanskruti eBooks (operated by SANSKRUTI SERVICE), the owner and operator of the website https://sanskrutiservice.in.

Sanskruti eBooks is an online digital goods retailer selling self-improvement, health, wellness, and Ayurvedic eBooks (PDF/ePub format) to customers in India.

For the purposes of applicable Indian privacy law, Sanskruti eBooks is the Data Controller / Business responsible for your personal information.

2 Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum information required to deliver your purchase and support you:

  • WhatsApp Mobile Number: Collected at checkout to send you your eBook download link after successful payment.
  • Name (Optional): Provided voluntarily via our review form or support messages.
  • City / Location (Optional): Provided voluntarily if you submit a reader review.
  • Review Content: Text you submit through our "Share Your Reading Experience" form, which may be displayed publicly on our website.
  • WhatsApp Lead Capture Number: If you submit your number in our "Download Free Sample Chapters" form, we collect it to send you the sample files.
  • Technical / Usage Data: Standard server logs may record your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time of visit for security and troubleshooting. We do not use this for marketing profiling.

We do not collect your full name, physical address, email address, Aadhaar number, PAN, or any government ID unless you directly share it with us via WhatsApp for support purposes.

3 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes only:

  • Order Fulfillment: To send your purchased eBook download links to your WhatsApp number immediately after successful payment.
  • Customer Support: To assist you with download issues, re-delivery requests, or any post-purchase queries via WhatsApp.
  • Free Sample Delivery: To send sample chapters to customers who voluntarily submit their WhatsApp number.
  • Review Display: To display your name, city, and review text on our website if you voluntarily submit a testimonial.
  • Service Improvement: Aggregated, anonymized usage data may be used to improve website performance and content.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable Indian laws and regulations.

We do not use your personal information for unsolicited marketing, advertising, or any purpose beyond what is listed above.

4 Payment Data & Processing

Sanskruti eBooks currently accepts payments via UPI (Unified Payments Interface) — including Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, and direct UPI ID / QR Code scan.

Important: Sanskruti eBooks does not collect, store, or process any payment card numbers, bank account details, UPI credentials, or any sensitive financial data. All UPI payments are processed directly through the payer's banking application or UPI app and are governed by the Reserve Bank of India (RBI) guidelines and your bank's own terms.

When you pay via UPI QR Code or UPI ID, the transaction happens entirely within your UPI app. We only receive a payment confirmation (either via WhatsApp notification from you, or via our order verification system) after the payment is made. We do not have access to your bank account or UPI PIN at any time.

Transaction records (amount, date, and UPI transaction reference number) may be retained for accounting, tax compliance, and refund processing purposes as required under Indian law.

5 Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for commercial purposes.

Your information may be shared in the following limited circumstances only:

  • WhatsApp Business API / Meta: Our customer support and order delivery operates through WhatsApp. By providing your WhatsApp number, your communication is subject to WhatsApp's own Privacy Policy.
  • Payment Processors: UPI transactions are processed by your own bank and UPI service provider. Sanskruti eBooks is not a party to those data flows beyond receiving payment confirmation.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by a court order, government authority, or applicable Indian law (including the Information Technology Act, 2000).
  • Business Transfer: If Sanskruti eBooks is acquired, merged, or transfers operations, customer data may be transferred as part of business assets, subject to this policy remaining in effect.

6 Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as reasonably necessary:

  • WhatsApp order records: Retained for up to 2 years for support and re-delivery purposes.
  • Review submissions: Retained indefinitely for public display unless you request removal.
  • Payment transaction references: Retained for 5–7 years as required under the Income Tax Act and GST regulations.
  • Technical logs: Retained for up to 90 days for security purposes.

You may request deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us via WhatsApp (+91 91830 53829). We will action reasonable deletion requests within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

7 Cookies & Tracking

Our website (sanskrutiservice.in) is a static single-page application. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics platforms (such as Google Analytics) at this time.

Your browser may store session data (such as your cart contents) in local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is used solely to preserve your shopping session. It is automatically cleared when you close your browser or clear browser data.

If we introduce analytics tools or third-party tracking in future, this policy will be updated and you will be notified via the website.

8 Your Rights

Under applicable Indian data protection principles (and in alignment with best practices), you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate personal data.
  • Deletion: Request erasure of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Objection: Object to our use of your data for any specific purpose.
  • Withdraw Consent: If you submitted your number for free sample chapters, you may request to be removed from future communications.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at +91 91830 53829 or email at paid32041@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 working days.

9 Data Security

We take reasonable and appropriate measures to protect your personal information from unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including:

  • Operating our website over HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption in transit).
  • Limiting access to personal data to only those who need it to operate the service.
  • Not storing payment credentials on our systems.

Note: No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your data has been compromised, contact us immediately.

10 Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for individuals aged 18 years and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, please contact us.

11 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, legal requirements, or services. When material changes are made, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

Continued use of our website and services after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
