STATUTORY DISCLOSURE & PAYMENT GATEWAY STATUS: Under the Public Gambling Act, state gaming enactments, the Consumer Protection Act, and payment aggregator (PayU/RBI) compliance frameworks, real-money participation in chance-based prize draws requires specific regulatory clearance and approved merchant underwriting.
